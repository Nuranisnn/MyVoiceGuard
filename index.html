ABOUT US

MyVoiceGuard is a platform designed to detect voice deepfakes and impersonations, with a focus on protecting Malaysians from misleading or manipulated audio content. Using advanced audio analysis and speaker verification, MyVoiceGuard identifies whether a voice recording is authentic, cloned, or impersonated. Our mission is to support digital safety and help reduce the spread of misinformation in Malaysia.
